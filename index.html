ABOUT CHARAN

Bringing people
together for life.

Charan is a community-focused blood donation camp initiative built around one simple belief: when we come together, we can help someone get another tomorrow.

Our goal is to make blood donation approachable, informed, and welcoming for every eligible donor. Whether it’s your first time or your tenth, your willingness to help matters.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We’re here.

A few quick answers for anyone thinking about donating.

Who can donate blood? +

Eligibility depends on your age, health, weight, medical history, and local rules. The camp’s qualified staff will assess your eligibility before donation.

Does donating blood hurt? +

You may feel a brief pinch when the needle is inserted. Staff will help you stay comfortable and answer any concerns.

How long does the whole visit take? +

Allow around 30–60 minutes for registration, screening, donation, and recovery. Timing can vary by camp and donor.

What should I do before donating? +

Follow the instructions from the blood-bank team. In general, eat a proper meal, drink water, and bring valid identification if requested.
